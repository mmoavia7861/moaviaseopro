Frequently asked questions

Is Meta advertising spend included?

No. Management fees are separate from the advertising budget charged by Meta.

Do you provide ad designs?

Creative direction and copy testing are included as described per plan. High-volume design, video production and third-party assets may require a separate scope.

Can you set up the Meta Pixel and CAPI?

We can configure or review supported integrations, given sufficient permissions and website or server access.

Are lead or sales results guaranteed?

No. Results depend on the product, offer, budgets, competition, landing pages and tracking quality.
