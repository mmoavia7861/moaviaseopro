Our capabilities

What's included in our seo services work

We tailor the following capabilities to the business model, available access and agreed package.

01

Complete SEO audits

A prioritized analysis of index coverage, site architecture, metadata, internal links, structured data, competitors, and search-intent gaps. Every audit concludes with an actionable issue list.

02

On-page optimization

Search-intent mapping, titles, descriptions, helpful page copy, header structure, internal linking, alt attributes, and content refreshes for priority URLs.

03

Technical SEO

Core Web Vitals diagnostics, crawling and rendering checks, robots directives, canonicals, redirects, XML sitemaps, schema validation, and mobile usability.

04

Off-page SEO & high-authority backlinks

Link-gap research, relevant publisher prospecting, digital PR and manual outreach for editorial backlinks. Domain Authority is a third-party score, not a Google ranking guarantee; we prioritize contextual relevance and quality.

05

Keyword and competitor research

Keyword clustering by intent and opportunity, competitor content comparisons, SERP feature review, and a practical content roadmap.

06

Local SEO

Google Business Profile guidance, local landing pages, NAP consistency checks where applicable, location-focused keyword research, and review-process recommendations.

07

E-commerce SEO

Category taxonomy, faceted navigation guidance, product schema, product page optimization, internal link flow, and large-catalog crawl control.

08

Measurement and reporting

Measurement plans using Google Search Console and Google Analytics where access is provided, including visibility, conversions, and completed deliverables.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How long does SEO take?

SEO timelines vary by site condition, competition, resources, and search demand. Technical fixes may be seen sooner, while organic growth often requires months of consistent work.

Do SEO packages include backlinks?

They include appropriate off-page analysis and outreach planning where scoped. Paid placements, third-party publishing fees, and guaranteed links are not included by default.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Search rankings depend on search engine systems and competitive factors beyond any agency’s control. We commit to transparent work and measurable reporting.

Which tools do you use?

Depending on access and project scope, audits can use Google Search Console, GA4, Screaming Frog, Semrush, Ahrefs and manual checks.
