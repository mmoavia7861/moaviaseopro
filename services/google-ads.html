Frequently asked questions

Does the monthly fee include ad spend?

No. You pay Google directly for ad spend, separately from the management fee.

Do you work with existing Google Ads accounts?

Yes. We can audit, restructure and optimize existing accounts with appropriate access.

Can you set up conversion tracking?

Yes, subject to website permissions, analytics consent requirements and supported integrations.

Can Google Ads guarantee a return on investment?

No. Performance depends on commercial factors, campaign data, market competition, budgets and website conversion experience.
