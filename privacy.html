Privacy policy

How this static website handles messages and basic site preferences.

Information you choose to share

Inquiry forms request your name, email, optional website, selected service and project details. The default form mode prepares a WhatsApp message on your device. The website itself does not receive or store that form data in this mode. The message is transmitted to Moavia SEO Pro only if you choose to press Send in WhatsApp, and WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices apply.

Netlify form hosting (optional)

If the owner changes the site to Netlify Forms mode and deploys there, form submissions are handled by Netlify and made available to the site owner. Any hosting provider may process standard service logs as described in its policies.

Support assistant

Our support assistant uses locally scripted replies. Chat text is processed in your browser; we do not send it to an AI model or store it on this website. Only a separate click on the WhatsApp link moves you to an external service.

Local storage and third-party links

The website may save a dark/light theme preference in browser local storage. Social, email and WhatsApp links lead to third-party platforms which apply their own policies. The website includes no analytics or advertising trackers by default.

Purpose and contact

Information you send us is used to respond to your inquiry, scope a service and communicate about it. For privacy questions, contact moaviahassan124@gmail.com.

Last updated: 20 October 2026. Replace or expand this policy when your actual hosting, analytics, consent, or form-processing arrangements change.
